AEye – AI-Based Exam Proctoring System

Multimodal Analysis of Visual Behavior for Cheating Detection in Computer-Based Exams. A dual-camera desktop app that watches gaze, head pose and posture during exams — designed for Philippine schools that can't afford commercial proctoring.

Each student calibrates with a short reading task, which trains their own Isolation Forest model, so the system learns what is normal for them instead of flagging everyone against one template. Student mode locks the screen to a kiosk browser on the LMS; proctor mode shows live alerts.

PythonMediaPipeYOLOv8Isolation ForestsolvePnP head poseMySQL

THREE-GATE FLAGGING RULE

1Model says the frame is anomalousper-student Isolation Forest
2Eyes are off the screeniris ratio + head pose
3It lasts at least 2 secondsone incident per episode
